# Privacy Policy for Selah - Bible & Devotional

**Last updated:** September 5, 2026

Welcome to **Selah - Bible & Devotional** ("Selah", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe, uplifting, and spiritually enriching experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you download, install, and use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Overview & Local-First Philosophy
Selah is designed as a **local-first** application. Core Bible and devotional features work offline, and your journal content is stored locally on your device. Purchases and purchase restoration require the appropriate App Store/Google Play services and network availability.

* All your reflections, devotional notes, reading progress, and bookmarks are stored **locally on your device**.
* Selah requires **no user account, no email registration, and no login**.
* You can export and backup your journal entries locally at any time as a JSON file.

---

### 2. Information We Collect and Why

#### A. In-App Purchase Information (Selah Grace Lifetime)
* **What is collected:** When you purchase **Selah Grace** (a one-time non-consumable lifetime purchase), transactions are processed directly by the **Google Play Store** or **Apple App Store**. 
* We do **not** collect or store your credit card numbers or financial details.
* We receive an anonymous transaction token, purchase status, and an anonymous app-user identifier strictly to unlock your lifetime features.
* **Service Provider:** We use **RevenueCat** to validate store receipts and manage lifetime purchase entitlements.

#### B. Device & Diagnostics Data
* Anonymous device information (such as device model, OS version, and app performance logs) may be collected to help us diagnose crashes and improve app stability.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
We partner with trusted platform providers strictly to enable store purchases and receipt verification:
* **Google Play In-App Billing / Apple In-App Purchase:** Processes store payments securely.
* **RevenueCat:** Validates digital receipts and entitlement status.

---

### 4. Advertisements & Tracking
* **No Ads:** Selah contains **no third-party advertisements**.
* **No Selling of Data:** We do **not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or journal entries to any advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Selah is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion
* **Local Data:** You can delete any journal entry, prayer, or reading history directly inside the app at any time. 
* Uninstalling the app completely removes all local database records and data from your device.
* You own your journal reflections and can export them as a standard JSON backup file at any time.
