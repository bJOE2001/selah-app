# Privacy Policy for Selah - Bible & Devotional

**Last updated:** September 5, 2026

Welcome to **Selah - Bible & Devotional** ("Selah", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe, uplifting, and spiritually enriching experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you download, install, and use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Overview & Local-First Philosophy
Selah is built on a **local-first** philosophy. Your personal spiritual journey, daily prayers, journal entries, and devotional reflections are private between you and God. 

* By default, all your reflections, devotional notes, reading progress, and bookmarks are stored **locally on your device**.
* You can use the core features of Selah completely offline without ever creating an account.

---

### 2. Information We Collect and Why

#### A. Optional Account Information (Cloud Vault)
* **What we collect:** If you choose to enable the optional **Encrypted Cloud Vault** to back up and sync your devotions across devices, we collect your **email address** and an encrypted password hash.
* **How it is protected:** Your journal notes are encrypted on your device prior to transmission. We cannot read the contents of your private reflections.
* **Service Provider:** We use **Supabase** to securely store your authentication credentials and encrypted backups.

#### B. Subscription & Purchase Information
* **What is collected:** When you subscribe to **Selah Grace** or purchase a pass, transactions are processed directly by the **Google Play Store**. 
* We do **not** collect or store your credit card numbers or financial details.
* We receive an anonymous transaction token, purchase status, and an anonymous user identifier to unlock your premium features.
* **Service Provider:** We use **RevenueCat** to validate store receipts and manage subscription entitlements.

#### C. Device & Diagnostics Data
* Anonymous device information (such as device model, OS version, and app performance logs) may be collected to help us diagnose crashes and improve app stability.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
We partner with trusted third-party providers strictly to enable core app functionality:
* **Google Play In-App Billing:** Processes subscriptions and payments securely.
* **RevenueCat:** Validates digital receipts and subscription status.
* **Supabase:** Provides encrypted cloud infrastructure for optional multi-device backup.

---

### 4. Advertisements & Tracking
* **No Ads:** Selah contains **no third-party advertisements**.
* **No Selling of Data:** We do **not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or journal entries to any advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Selah is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

---

### 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion
* **Local Data:** You can delete any journal entry, prayer, or reading history directly inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app completely removes all local data from your device.
* **Cloud Vault Data:** If you have registered a Cloud Vault account and wish to delete your account and all associated cloud backups, you may request full deletion at any time by contacting us at the email address below.
